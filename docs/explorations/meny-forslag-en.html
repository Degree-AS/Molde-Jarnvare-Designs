Product menu

Department menu (A) - for developers

Proposal A was chosen on 20.11.2026. The demo below is clickable and runs on Molde Jarnvare's real category tree from /mj/web/api/navigation/menu?areaId=27: 15 main categories, 125 subcategories and 445 on the third level. The explanations are in English. The menu copy stays in Norwegian because that is what ships, and there is a glossary at the bottom. Proposals B and C were dropped (C because the site search already does the job) and are only on the Norwegian page.

Update 23.11.2026: build the menu and the content area now, without «Dine kategorier». MJ wants more clarity on it first, so it is specified and estimated later as a separate change. The sections about it stay on this page as input for that, marked «later». In the demo, «Build now» shows what ships.

Why today's menu does not work

A wall of 125 links

«Produkter» opens all 15 main categories with every subcategory at once, equally weighted, in one panel that has to be scrolled.

UPPER CASE FROM BC

Names are shown as they are in BC. All-caps text is slower to read and looks like shouting.

445 categories are invisible

The third level («Sekskantskruer», «Spiralbor (HSS)») is not shown, yet that is where professionals actually shop.

The menu knows nothing about the customer

Trade customers buy the same things again and again, but the menu looks the same for everyone.

A

Department menu Chosen 20.11.2026

The main categories sit in a fixed rail on the left. The one you point at opens all its level 2 and level 3 subcategories in the panel next to it, with a content area for campaigns and services on the right. «Dine kategorier» (your categories) for logged-in customers comes later as a separate change. Mobile: a drill-down menu with the categories directly on the first level.

Why this is a good choice

  • 15 choices instead of 125. One short list with icon and count, then level 2 and 3 for one category at a time.
  • All subcategories visible, no «+ N more». Four columns fit everything in 14 of 15 categories without scrolling. Verktøy og redskap (113 links) gets the full width and five columns.
  • Content area per main category, managed in the CMS: campaign or news on top, then the services and supply solutions that belong to the category.
  • Later, separate change - your categories for logged-in customers: the customer's regular categories from their BC orders, one click away. Subcategories they bought from get a dot in every department.
  • Well-known pattern from Grainger and Home Depot, with a 300 ms hover delay so it does not flicker.

Inspired by

Grainger, Home Depot (rail + panel, «Buy It Again»), Digi-Key (counts), McMaster-Carr (specs as navigation).

Downsides

A rail shows only one department's content at a time; a customer who does not know where an item lives has to point through several. The search and «Dine kategorier» reduce this. The content area needs a CMS field per main category that someone keeps up to date. On mobile, NN/g warns against drill-down menus, so the back button must name the level («‹ Festemidler»).

    Behaviour

    Desktop

    • Hovering a rail item switches the panel after 300 ms, so the mouse can move diagonally to the panel without switching. Click and keyboard focus switch at once; arrow up/down moves between rail items.
    • Festemidler is open when the menu opens. (Later, with «Dine kategorier»: that is the first rail item and is open instead.)
    • All level 3 links are shown under their level 2 heading in a 4-column layout. Level 2 headings are links too.
    • Over 90 links in a department: the groups take the full width in 5 columns and the content area moves below.
    • The panel head has «Se alle N produkter» for the department. (Later: «Dere har kjøpt fra N av disse» when the customer has bought from it.)
    • Labels are shown in sentence case; abbreviations stay upper case (DIN, ISO, HSS, SDS, TIG, MIG/MAG, MMA, PTFE, LOTO).

    Mobile

    • First level: search field, then the 15 main categories with icon and count. (Later: «Dine kategorier» as a horizontally scrolling row of chips above them, logged in only.)
    • Each level starts with «Se alle i …» and the back button names the parent level.
    • Later, with «Dine kategorier»: dots show the way down: on a level 2 item that holds a category the customer bought from, and on that level 3 category itself.

    Content area (CMS)

    • Per main category: an optional campaign (image, tag, title, text, link) and 0-3 links to services or supply solutions.
    • Without a campaign, only the links are shown. Without either, a default (contact a product manager).
    • Later: in «Dine kategorier» the campaign is chosen from the existing CMS campaigns, by the customer's categories («I dine kategorier»).

    «Dine kategorier» (later, separate change): data, storage and load

    Source: already in Dynamicweb

    BC order sync (AB#20048) copies the BC orders of every web shop customer into EcomOrders / EcomOrderLines, including orders not placed on the web, with posted invoices for the last 730 days (BcOrderSyncSettings.ImportFromDays). No new BC integration is needed. The owner rule can be the same as in MyOrdersReader: OrderCustomerNumber, or the user id when there is no customer number.

    Computed nightly, stored small

    • One SQL aggregate per night, e.g. as a step after the nightly full BC order sync, or as its own scheduled task.
    • Order lines → the product's primary group (level 3) → grouped per customer.
    • Count orders, not quantity, or 1 000 screws always beat one drill. Weight by recency, e.g. 1.0 for the last 3 months, 0.5 within a year, 0.25 older.
    • Keep the top 8-12 per customer. Skip inactive products and groups no longer in the menu.
    • A few thousand customers × 12 rows is a tiny table.

    Must not slow the menu down

    • The menu tree stays the same for everyone and is cached as today (navigation:menu / pages:area:N tags).
    • «Dine kategorier» is a separate small call (~1 KB) made only when logged in, after the menu has rendered. It reads precomputed rows for one customer and can be cached per session.
    • Nothing is counted per category when the menu renders. The per-category product counts in the side menu caused the bursts of 585 queries and the PROD outage on 19-20.11 (AB#20648). The product counts in the rail come from the one query in PR 5549.
    -- Sketch only: table and column names to be confirmed, especially how the primary group is stored.
-- Nightly: rebuild MjCustomerCategories (CustomerNumber, GroupId, Orders, LastPurchased, Score, Rank)
WITH bought AS (            -- one row per customer, group and order, however many lines
  SELECT DISTINCT o.OrderCustomerNumber AS CustomerNumber, g.GroupId, o.OrderId, o.OrderCompletedDate AS Day
  FROM EcomOrders o
  JOIN EcomOrderLines l ON l.OrderLineOrderId = o.OrderId
  JOIN <primary group of l.OrderLineProductId> g      -- the product's level 3 group
  WHERE o.OrderComplete = 1 AND o.OrderCart = 0 AND ISNULL(o.OrderDeleted, 0) = 0
    AND o.OrderCompletedDate >= DATEADD(month, -24, GETDATE())
), scored AS (
  SELECT CustomerNumber, GroupId,
         COUNT(*) AS Orders,
         MAX(Day) AS LastPurchased,
         SUM(CASE WHEN Day >= DATEADD(month, -3, GETDATE()) THEN 1.0
                  WHEN Day >= DATEADD(month, -12, GETDATE()) THEN 0.5 ELSE 0.25 END) AS Score
  FROM bought GROUP BY CustomerNumber, GroupId
)
SELECT *, ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY CustomerNumber ORDER BY Score DESC, LastPurchased DESC) AS Rank
FROM scored                 -- keep Rank <= 12

    Endpoint (suggestion)

    For example GET /mj/web/api/account/my-categories, returning up to 12 items: groupId, label path (L1 › L2 › L3), url, orders, lastPurchased. Private, never cached across users. When a salesperson acts on behalf of a customer, it returns the acting customer's categories. Customers with no orders get an empty list, and the menu hides the section.

    Chosen 20.11.2026

    Department menu (A)

    Known from the largest B2B shops, shows every subcategory and fixes the wall of links. Can be built in steps.

    Order

    1. Now, with today's data: rail and panel with all subcategories, sentence case and icons. Drill-down menu on mobile.
    2. Content area: a CMS field per main category for campaign, news and services.
    3. Later, separate change - Dine kategorier: nightly aggregate, small table and endpoint, menu section on desktop and mobile. Not in this build; clarified, specified and estimated on its own.
    4. Before launch: a short test with 5 customers: find hex screws, work gloves and a hydraulic hose.
